    Description

    Khaman is a soft, fluffy, and savory steamed snack from Gujarat, made from split chickpea flour (besan). It is tangy, sweet, and lightly spiced, then topped with a sizzling mustard seed tempering, fresh coriander, and grated coconut.

    Ingredients

    • 1 cup besan(gram flour)
    • 1 tsp ginger-green chillie paste
    • 1 tbsp lemon juice
    • 1 tsp sugar
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1 tsp eno(fruit salt)
    • 1 cup water
    • 1 tbps oil

    Tampering ingredients

    • 1 tbsp oil
    • 1 tbsp mustard seeds
    • 1 tsp sesame seeds
    • 2 slit green chillies
    • A pinch of asafoetida
    • 1/2 cup water
    • 2 tsp sugar

    Steps

    1. Whisk the besan, ginger-green chili paste, lemon juice, sugar, salt, oil, and water in a bowl to make a smooth, lump-free batter.
    2. Preheat your steamer with water and grease a baking pan or dhokla tray with a little oil.
    3. Add the Eno fruit salt to the batter, pour a teaspoon of water over it to activate, and quickly mix in one direction until frothy.
    4. Immediately pour the frothy batter into the greased pan and place it in the preheated steamer.
    5. Cover and steam on medium-high heat for 12 to 15 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
    6. Remove the pan from the steamer, let it cool for 5 minutes, and slice the khaman into neat squares.
    7. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a small pan, add the mustard seeds and sesame seeds, and let them crackle.
    8. Add the slit green chillies, asafoetida, water, and sugar to the pan, bringing the mixture to a quick boil.
    9. Pour the warm tempering liquid evenly over the sliced khaman pieces so they absorb the water and turn soft.
    10. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander leaves and grated coconut before serving.
