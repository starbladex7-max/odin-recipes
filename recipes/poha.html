    • Poha recipe


    Description

    Poha is a quick, light, and savory Indian breakfast made from flattened rice flakes

    Tossed with mustard seeds, onions, chilies, and peanuts, it is ready in under 20 minutes

    ingredients

    • 1.5 cups thick flattened rice (poha)
    • 2 tablespoons oil
    • 3 tablespoons peanuts
    • 0.75 teaspoon mustard seeds
    • A few curry leaves
    • 1 chopped onion
    • 1-2 chopped green chilies
    • 0.25 teaspoon turmeric powder
    • Salt and a pinch of sugar (to taste)
    • Fresh coriander leaves and lemon juice for garnish

    steps

    1. Wash Poha: Rinse the flakes in a strainer under running water until soft, then drain completely.
    2. Fry peanuts: Heat oil in pan and fry the peanuts until crunchy; set them aside.
    3. Sauté Spices: Add mustard seeds and curry leaves to the remaining hot oil until they crackle.
    4. Cook Onions: Add onions and green chillies, Cook until soft.
    5. Mix Poha: Stir in turmeric, salt, sugar, and the drained poha. Toss well.
    6. Finish: Mix in fried peanuts, turn off the heat, and add fresh lemon juice and coriander
